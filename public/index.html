An alternative UX for Emacs Calc

Work on the math under point.

Move to part of a formula, press a command, and the result goes back in place. maf brings direct editing and contextual commands to Calc, with a highlight showing what each command will act on.

A few things you can do

Act on any part of a formula

Use the same commands on an entry, a sub-expression, a selection, or a run of terms. Map a command over a vector or both sides of an equation.

How targeting works →

Edit the stack as text

SPC opens the stack for editing. Type a new formula or change an existing one, then press RET. Split and join entries with newlines.

Learn the editing gesture →

See your work

Preview formulas in two dimensions, plot below the stack, and browse earlier stack states. Optional typesetting renders formulas as SVG.

Explore display and history modules →

Make it your own

Choose native, Calc, or Vim keys. Toggle modules as you work, and look up commands with descriptions, examples, and flag variants.
