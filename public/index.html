maf makes Emacs Calc act on the part of the formula under point.

Put point on the ^ and press a x. Only that side expands.

1:  (x + 1)^2 = 9
1:  x^2 + 2 x + 1 = 9

On the home line at the bottom, a command acts on the top entry, like Calc always did. Inside a formula, it acts on the sub-formula under point, which is highlighted. On an equation, it runs once per side. With a region marked, it acts on just those terms. The result replaces exactly that, and point stays put.

SPC opens the stack as text. Type, then RET. On an entry, SPC edits it in place. That one gesture is how everything is entered and changed.

Emacs 28.1 or later, nothing else.

M-x package-vc-install RET https://github.com/quxbaz/emacs-maf RET
(add-hook 'calc-mode-hook #'maf-mode)

Then M-x calc. Inside, l b lists every key with an example. Source on GitHub. Under active development; the API is unstable.
