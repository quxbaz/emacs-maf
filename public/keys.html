Key bindings

Every command maf binds in a calc buffer, grouped by what it does. Hover or focus a key to see its example, its description, and what the I and H flags turn it into. Click to pin.

dot = contextual: acts on what point and the calc state resolve to (home, entry, selection, sub-expression, or each side of an equation) and writes the result back there. I inverse flag · H hyperbolic flag · IH both, pressed before the key. M map flag: every contextual command runs once per element of a vector, or once per side of a relation.
